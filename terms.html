What Skry is

Skry is an entertainment and reflection app. Readings are generated from measurements of a photograph you take. They are not predictions, and they are not advice of any kind — medical, psychological, financial, legal or otherwise. Do not make decisions on the basis of a reading.

Subscriptions and credits

Skry+ is offered as a weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, and credits are offered as a one-time purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation, except during an eligible free trial. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

Eligible customers can receive a 3-day trial on the weekly plan or a 2-week trial on the yearly plan. Apple determines eligibility for this subscription group and shows the applicable offer before purchase. A trial renews into the selected paid plan unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. Credit packs do not include a subscription trial.

Apple's standard licence applies: Licensed Application End User Licence Agreement.

Acceptable use

Do not use Skry to photograph people with the intent of analysing them; Skry declines photos containing faces on your device. Do not attempt to abuse the reading service or circumvent its rate limits.

Availability

Photo analysis and reading generation run on your device in Skry 1.5. App Store purchases and restoring subscriptions require a connection to Apple.

Skry nedir

Skry bir eğlence ve düşünme uygulamasıdır. Okumalar, çektiğin bir fotoğrafın ölçümlerinden üretilir. Bunlar kehanet değildir ve hiçbir türden tavsiye değildir — tıbbi, psikolojik, finansal, hukuki ya da başka. Bir okumaya dayanarak karar verme.
