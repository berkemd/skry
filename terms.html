What Skry is

Skry is an entertainment and reflection app. Readings are generated from measurements of a photograph you take. They are not predictions, and they are not advice of any kind — medical, psychological, financial, legal or otherwise. Do not make decisions on the basis of a reading.

Subscriptions and credits

Skry+ is offered as a weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, and credits are offered as a one-time purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

The weekly plan includes a 3-day free trial, available once per Apple Account. The trial converts to a paid weekly subscription unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. If you buy a subscription while a trial is running, the unused part of the trial is forfeited. The yearly plan and the credit pack do not include a trial.

Apple's standard licence applies: Licensed Application End User Licence Agreement.

Acceptable use

Do not use Skry to photograph people with the intent of analysing them; Skry declines photos containing faces on your device. Do not attempt to abuse the reading service or circumvent its rate limits.

Availability

The online wording service may be unavailable at times. Skry continues to work fully offline; no functionality depends on a connection.
